Protein Packed BBQ Chicken Salad

Description

This protein-packed BBQ chicken salad is loaded with bold barbecue flavor and couldn't be easier to make. Simply toss everything together in one bowl with a creamy Greek yogurt and mayonnaise dressing flavored with BBQ rub and BBQ sauce for an easy lunch or meal-prep favorite.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups shredded and chopped cooked chicken breast
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped red onion
  • 1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup BBQ sauce
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 3 tablespoons dill relish
  • 1 teaspoon BBQ rub seasoning

Steps

  1. Combine chicken, red onion, jalapeno, BBQ sauce, cilantro, yogurt, mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, relish, and BBQ rub in a large bowl and mix well. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes or until ready to serve.
